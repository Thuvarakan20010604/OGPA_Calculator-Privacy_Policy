Privacy Policy for OGPA Calculator

Effective Date: January 2026

OGPA Calculator does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information with us or any third parties.

The app stores student name, subject names, credits, grades, GPA and OGPA calculation data locally on the user's device only.

The app allows users to generate PDF reports and local backup files. These files are created and stored locally on the user's device.

The app does not use analytics, advertising networks, user tracking services, or external databases.
